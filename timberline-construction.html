Questions

Timber, answered plainly.

Is a tall timber building safe in a fire?

Thick engineered timber chars slowly on the outside and keeps its strength inside. Every design is fire-tested and approved by the authority before we build.

Does it cost more than concrete?

Usually the same within a few percent. A shorter programme and lighter foundations often pay back the difference.

How tall can you go?

Our current limit is 18 storeys in timber, and taller with a hybrid timber and concrete core.

How long do the buildings last?

We design for at least 100 years, with an inspection and maintenance plan handed over at completion.
