      Questions

      What people ask before they sign.

      Do you give a fixed price?

      Yes. After design approval we sign a detailed bill of quantities. The price changes only if you ask for changes to the design.

      Are your housing projects RERA registered?

      Every housing scheme we sell is registered under RERA, and the registration number is shown on all material.

      Can I build on my own plot?

      Yes. Bungalows and small commercial buildings on your own land are a large part of our work.

      How long does a bungalow take?

      A 3,000 sq ft bungalow usually takes 8 to 10 months from foundation to handover, depending on design and monsoon.

      What warranty do you give?

      One year of free defect repair after handover, and structural guidance for the life of the building.
