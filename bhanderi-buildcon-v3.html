      Questions

      What people ask before they sign.

      How are your rates fixed?

      Government work is quoted against the department’s schedule of quantities. We bid at a fixed percentage, and the rate holds for the whole contract unless the department approves extra items.

      How do you control quality?

      Third-party testing of materials, hydro and leakage tests on pipelines and cube tests on concrete, with the records attached to every measurement book.

      Do you take private or industrial infrastructure work?

      Yes. Industrial estates, townships and large layouts hire us for internal roads, drains, water lines and treatment units.

      How long does a typical project take?

      A 5 km water-supply or sewer package usually takes 8 to 12 months from work order to commissioning, depending on soil, traffic and monsoon. Treatment plants take longer.

      What happens after handover?

      We stay through the defect-liability period set in the contract, repair defects free of charge, and hand the department as-built drawings and test records.

      Do you take government and municipal tenders?

      Yes. We bid for water supply, sewerage, drainage, treatment plant and road works, and handle the technical and financial documents, quality testing and measurement-book billing the departments require.
