Questions

What clients ask before they sign.

Do you work with our architect?

Yes. We build from your architect's design, or provide a design-build team under one contract. You choose.

How is the price fixed?

After design freeze we give a guaranteed maximum price. Savings found on site are shared with you.

What size of project do you take?

From about $5 million to $400 million. Smaller heritage and specialist work is considered case by case.

How soon can you start?

Pre-construction can begin within three weeks of a signed letter of intent.
